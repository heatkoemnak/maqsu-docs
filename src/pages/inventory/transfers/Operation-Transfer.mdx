A **Transfer Operation** in inventory management refers to the process of moving products between warehouses or internal locations within the same organization. In **MAQSU ERP**, these transfers help you keep real-time track of your stock levels across all locations.

### Why you see multiple lines for one PO

![](/img/inventory/transfer/2.png)

This warehouse flow is:

`Vendor` → `Input` → `Quality Control` → `Stock`

>So MAQSU automatically creates 3 pickings per receipt.

**Example: PO P00003**

1. WH-03/IN/00003— Vendor → Input

    - From: Partner Locations / Vendors

    - To: WH-03 / Input

    - Status: **Ready**
        - 👉 Goods were ready to receive from the vendor

2. WH-03/IN/00005 — Input → Quality Control

    - From: WH-03 / Input

    - To: WH-03 / Quality Control

    - Status: **Waiting for another Operation**
        - 👉 Goods are waiting for the previous step (Vendor → Input)

3. WH-03/INT/00006 — Quality Control → Stock

    - From: WH-03 / Quality Control

    - To: WH-03 / Stock

    - Status: **Waiting Another Operation**
        -👉 This transfer is blocked until QC step is completed
---
### Understanding “Waiting Another Operation”

**"Waiting Another Operation"** means this transfer is **on hold** until a required earlier transfer is completed.

**Example:**

- The **QC → Stock** transfer cannot proceed (waiting).
- The **Input → QC** transfer must be completed first.

>Think of it as a relay race—each step waits for the baton to be passed along!

### Why Use Operation Transfer?

- **Balance Stock Levels**: Move items from overflowing locations to those in need.
- **Fulfill Orders Faster**: Relocate products closer to where they are required.
- **Maintain Accurate Inventory**: Prevent discrepancies by documenting all internal product movements.

## How to Perform an Operation Transfer

### 1. **Inventory > Operations > Transfers**  
>![](/img/inventory/transfer/1.png)

### 2. **Click "Transfer line"**  
   Begin a new transfer operation.
>![](/img/inventory/transfer/3.png)

### 3. **Fill In the Transfer Details:**
   - **Operation Type:** Select *Internal Transfer*.
   - **Source Location:** Choose the warehouse or location where products are currently stored.
   - **Destination Location:** Choose where you want to move the products.
   - **Scheduled Date:** Select when the transfer should take place.

### 4. **Add Products to Transfer:**  
   - Enter each product and its quantity to transfer.

### 5. **Verify & Confirm:**  
   - Review all details and click **Confirm** to validate the transfer.

6. **Process the Transfer:**  
   - MAQSU may require steps like picking, packing, or validating the move depending on your configuration.

7. **Done:**  
   Once validated, your inventory levels will update to reflect the move.

---

## Example

Suppose you want to move 20 units of Product *A* from Warehouse 1 to Warehouse 2:

- **Source Location:** Warehouse 1 / Stock
- **Destination Location:** Warehouse 2 / Stock
- **Product:** Product A
- **Quantity:** 20

After following the steps above and confirming, *20 units* will be subtracted from Warehouse 1 and added to Warehouse 2.

---

> **Tip:**  
> Always ensure both source and destination locations are correctly configured in the system to prevent errors during transfers.
