      A **Request for Quotation (RFQ)** is the first supplier document in the purchase flow. Use it when you need to request prices, quantities, or order details from a vendor before confirming a purchase order.

          Use the following steps to create a request for quotation:

          ##### Step 1 — Navigate

          From the left sidebar, click on **Purchase** to open the Purchase
          module. Then, from the top navigation menu, go to **Orders** ‣
          **Requests for Quotation**.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0001.png)

          Once you navigate to **Requests for Quotation**, you will see the
          master list of all existing RFQs. The list displays the following
          columns:

          1. **RFQ Number** – The unique document number automatically assigned
          to each request (e.g., P00008, P00009).

          2. **Order Date** – The date and time the RFQ was created.

          3. **Vendor** – The name of the supplier associated with the request.

          4. **Reference** – An optional reference identifier for internal
          tracking.

          5. **Purchase Representative** – The MAQSU user responsible for
          managing this request.

          6. **Total** – The total value of the RFQ.

          7. **Status** – The current status of the document, such as **RFQ** or
          **Purchase Order**.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0002.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
            You can use the **Filters**, **Group By**, and **Search** bar at the top right to quickly find specific records. You can also switch between **List**, **Kanban**, and **Calendar** views using the view icons.
          </Noted>

          ##### Step 2 — Click on the Create button

          To start a new purchase request, click the **Create** button at the
          top-left of the Requests for Quotation list page.


          This will open a blank **Request for Quotation** form ready for you to
          fill in.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0003.png)

          ##### Step 3 — Complete the Fields

          Once the form is open in **New** status, complete the following fields:

          1. **Vendor** *(Required)* – Search and select the supplier by name,
          TIN, email, or reference. This field identifies who you are sending
          the quotation request to. Start typing and select the correct vendor
          from the dropdown list.

          2. **Reference** *(Optional)* – Enter an internal reference number or
          note to help identify this request. This is useful for linking the RFQ
          to an internal purchase requisition or project.

          3. **Doc. No.** – This field is automatically generated by the system.
          You can fill this in manually if you have your own document number.

          4. **Order Date** – This is automatically set to the current date and
          time. You can adjust it if needed.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0004.png)

          Under the **Order Lines** tab, add the products or services you wish
          to request:

          * Click **Add a product** to add a new line item.

          * Fill in the **Product**, **Description**, **Quantity**, **UOM**,
          **Unit Price**, **Discount**, and **Taxes** for each line.

          * The **Subtotal** will be calculated automatically.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0005.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Tip" image="/img/icon/tips.png">
            You can also click **Add a section** to group your order lines, or **Add a note** to include internal remarks on a specific line.
          </Noted>

          ##### Step 4 — Save the Purchase Request

          After filling in all the required fields, click the **Save** button at
          the top-left to save your new Purchase Request for Quotation.

          Once saved, the system will assign a **Doc. No.** automatically and
          the RFQ will appear in the master list with the status **RFQ**.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0006.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Next step" image="/img/icon/next.png">
            After saving, you can send the RFQ to your supplier by clicking **Send by Email**, or confirm it directly into a **Purchase Order** by clicking **Confirm Order**.
          </Noted>

        body: |
          Once a Purchase Order has been confirmed, the next step is to receive
          the goods from the supplier. Follow the steps below to complete the
          goods receiving process in MAQSU ERP.

          ##### Step 1 — Go to Purchase Orders

          From the left sidebar, click **Purchase**. Then from the top
          navigation menu, go to **Orders** ‣ **Purchase Orders**.

          ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0001.png)

          ##### Step 2 — Find the Purchase Order

          You need to find the Purchase Order you want to receive goods for. Use
          the **Filters** to narrow down the list quickly.

          Click the **Filters** button at the top-right of the list, then look
          for **Billing Status is "Nothing to Bill"** from the filter options
          and click it.

          ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0002.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Can't find the filter?"
          image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            If **Billing Status is "Nothing to Bill"** does not appear in the standard filter list, you can use a **Custom Filter** instead. Click **Add Custom Filter**, then set the conditions as follows:

            1. **Field:** Billing Status
            2. **Operator:** is
            3. **Value:** Nothing to Bill

            Then click **Apply** to apply the custom filter.
          </Noted>

          ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0003.png)

          After applying the filter, the list will show only Purchase Orders
          with a **Billing Status** of **Nothing to Bill**.

          ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0004.png)

          ##### Step 3 — Open the Purchase Order

          Click on the **Order Number** of the Purchase Order you want to
          receive goods for to open it.

          You will see the Purchase Order detail page showing the vendor, order
          date, and the **Order Lines** with the ordered products, quantities,
          and amounts. Note that the **Received** column will show **0.00**,
          indicating that no goods have been received yet.

          ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0005.png)

          ##### Step 4 — Click Receive Products

          Click the **Receive Products** button at the top of the Purchase Order
          form to start the goods receiving process.

          This will open the **Goods Receipt** form (e.g., WH/IN/00004) which
          shows all products from the Purchase Order that are pending receipt.

          ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0006.png)

          The Goods Receipt form displays:

          * **Receive From** – The vendor supplying the goods.

          * **Operation Type** – The warehouse operation type, typically **Goods
          Receipts**.

          * **Scheduled Date** – The expected date of receipt.

          * **Source Document** – The Purchase Order number this receipt is
          linked to.

          * **Operations Tab** – Lists each product with its **Demand** (ordered
          quantity) and **Done** (received quantity).

          <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            The status bar at the top right shows the current stage of the receipt: **Draft → Waiting → Ready → Done**. When you open the receipt, it will be in **Ready** status, meaning the goods are ready to be validated.
          </Noted>

          ##### Step 5 — Click Validate

          After reviewing the goods receipt, click the **Validate** button to
          confirm that the goods have been physically received.

          ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0007.png)

          ##### Step 6 — Click Apply on the Confirmation Dialog

          A confirmation dialog — **Immediate Transfer?** — will appear. This
          dialog informs you that you have not yet recorded the **Done**
          quantities manually, and that clicking **Apply** will instruct MAQSU
          to automatically process all quantities as received.

          Click **Apply** to confirm and complete the goods receipt.

          ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0008.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            If you want to record partial receipts or specify exact done quantities manually before validating, click **Cancel** on this dialog, update the **Done** column for each product, then click **Validate** again.
          </Noted>

          ##### Step 7 — Verify the Status Changed to Done

          After clicking **Apply**, the goods receipt status will automatically
          update to **Done**. You will also notice that:

          * The **Done** column now shows the received quantities matching the
          **Demand**.

          * An **Effective Date** has been recorded, showing the exact date and
          time the goods were received.

          * The available action buttons change to **Return**, **Scrap**, and
          **Unlock**, confirming the receipt is complete.

          ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0009.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Next Step" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            Once goods are received, the next step is to **Create a Vendor Bill** to record the supplier invoice and process payment against the Purchase Order.
          </Noted>

    body: |
      Receiving goods is a critical step in the purchasing process in MAQSU ERP.
      It confirms that the ordered products have been physically delivered by
      the supplier, updates your inventory stock levels, and prepares the
      Purchase Order for vendor billing.


      <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
        Always verify that the quantities and products received match the Purchase Order
        before validating the goods receipt. Any discrepancies should be resolved with the
        supplier before completing the process.
      </Noted>

        body: |
          After goods have been received, the next step is to create a **Vendor
          Bill** to record the supplier invoice and process payment. Follow the
          steps below:

          ##### Step 1 — Go to Purchase Orders

          From the left sidebar, click **Purchase**. Then from the top
          navigation menu, go to **Orders** ‣ **Purchase Orders**.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0001.png)

          ##### Step 2 — Find the Purchase Order

          You need to find the Purchase Order that is ready for billing. Use the
          **Filters** to narrow down the list quickly.

          Click the **Filters** button at the top-right of the list, then select
          **Billing Status is "Waiting Bills"** from the filter options.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0002.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Can't find the filter?"
          image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            If **Billing Status is "Waiting Bills"** does not appear in the standard filter list, use a **Custom Filter** instead. Click **Add Custom Filter**, then set the conditions as follows:

            1. **Field:** Billing Status
            2. **Operator:** is
            3. **Value:** Waiting Bills

            Then click **Apply** to apply the custom filter.
          </Noted>

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0003.png)

          After applying the filter, the list will display only Purchase Orders
          with a **Billing Status** of **Waiting Bills**.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0004.png)

          ##### Step 3 — Open the Purchase Order

          Click on the **Order Number** of the Purchase Order you want to create
          a vendor bill for to open it.

          On the Purchase Order detail page, verify that goods have been
          received by checking the **Received** column in the Order Lines — it
          should now show the received quantity (e.g., 100.00). The **Billed**
          column will still show **0.00**, indicating no bill has been created
          yet.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0005.png)

          ##### Step 4 — Click Create Bill

          Click the **Create Bill** button at the top of the Purchase Order form
          to start the billing process.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0006.png)

          ##### Step 5 — Choose Bill Type and Confirm

          A **Create Bill** dialog will appear. The bill will be created in
          **Draft** status so you can review it before posting. Select the
          appropriate bill type:

          1. **Regular Bill** *(Default)* – Creates a standard vendor bill for
          the full amount of the purchase order. Use this for normal purchases.

          2. **Down Payment (Percentage)** – Creates a partial bill based on a
          percentage of the total purchase order amount. Use this when paying a
          deposit upfront.

          3. **Down Payment (Fixed Amount)** – Creates a partial bill for a
          specific fixed amount. Use this when the advance payment is a set
          amount rather than a percentage.

          Select the appropriate option, then click **Create and View Bill** to
          create the bill and open it immediately.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0007.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            If you click **Create Bill** instead of **Create and View Bill**, the bill will be created in the background without opening it. You can find it later under the **Accounting** module → **Vendor Bills**.
          </Noted>

          ##### Step 6 — Review the Draft Bill and Click Post

          The **Draft Bill** form will open automatically. Review all the
          details carefully before posting:

          1. **Vendor** – The supplier linked to this bill.

          2. **Bill Date** – The date the bill was created.

          3. **Accounting Date** – The date used for accounting entries.

          4. **Payment Terms** – The agreed payment due date.

          5. **Journal** – The accounting journal, typically **Vendor Bills
          (USD)**.

          6. **Invoice Lines** – Lists all products from the Purchase Order with
          their quantities, unit prices, accounts, and subtotals.


          Once you have verified all the information is correct, click the
          **Post** button to validate and post the vendor bill. This will change
          the status from **Draft** to **Posted** and record the corresponding
          journal entries in your accounting.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0008.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Next Step" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            Once the vendor bill is posted, the next step is to **Register Payment** to record the actual payment made to the supplier against this bill.
          </Noted>

    body: |

        <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
          A vendor bill can only be created after the goods have been
          received and the Purchase Order billing status is **Waiting Bills**.
          Always verify the invoice details match the Purchase Order before posting
          the bill.

          After the vendor bill has been posted, the final step in the
          purchasing process is to **Register Payment** to record the actual
          payment made to the supplier. Follow the steps below:

          ##### Step 1 — Go to Purchase Orders

          From the left sidebar, click **Purchase**. Then from the top
          navigation menu, go to **Orders** ‣ **Purchase Orders**.

          ![](/img/purchase/register-payment/0001.png)


          ##### Step 2 — Find the Purchase Order

          You need to find the Purchase Order that has been fully billed. Use
          the **Filters** to narrow down the list quickly.

          Click the **Filters** button at the top-right of the list, then select
          **Billing Status is "Fully Billed"** from the filter options.

          ![](/img/purchase/register-payment/0002.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Can't find the filter?"
          image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            If **Billing Status is "Fully Billed"** does not appear in the standard filter list, use a **Custom Filter** instead. Click **Add Custom Filter**, then set the conditions as follows:

            1. **Field:** Billing Status
            2. **Operator:** is
            3. **Value:** Fully Billed

            Then click **Apply** to apply the custom filter.
          </Noted>

          ![](/img/purchase/register-payment/0003.png)

          After applying the filter, the list will display only Purchase Orders
          with a **Billing Status** of **Fully Billed**.

          ![](/img/purchase/register-payment/0004.png)

          ##### Step 3 — Open the Purchase Order

          Click on the **Order Number** of the Purchase Order you want to
          register payment for to open it.

          ![](/img/purchase/register-payment/0009.png)

          ##### Step 3 — Click the Vendor Bills Button

          On the Purchase Order detail page, you can confirm that both goods
          have been received and billed by checking the **Received** and
          **Billed** columns in the Order Lines — both should now show matching
          quantities (e.g., 100.00). You will also see the **1 Vendor Bills**
          smart button at the top right of the form.

          Click the **1 Vendor Bills** smart button at the top right of the
          Purchase Order form. This will open the posted vendor bill linked to
          this purchase order.

          ![](/img/purchase/register-payment/0005.png)

          The Vendor Bill (e.g., BILL/2026/0004) will open in **Posted** status.
          You will see the bill details including the vendor, bill date,
          accounting date, payment terms, journal, and invoice lines. The **Add
          Payment** button will be visible at the top of the form.

          ##### Step 4 — Click Add Payment

          Click the **Add Payment** button at the top of the Vendor Bill form to
          open the payment dialog.

          ![](/img/purchase/register-payment/0006.png)

          A payment wizard window will appear. Fill in the following details:

          1. **Journal** – Select the payment method or account the payment is
          made from (e.g., Bank (USD), Cash). This determines which financial
          account is debited.

          2. **Doc. No.** – This is automatically generated by the system. You
          can leave it as is or enter a custom reference such as a check number
          or bank transfer ID.

          3. **Amount** – The payment amount is automatically pre-filled based
          on the outstanding balance on the bill. You can adjust this for a
          partial payment if needed.

          4. **Payment Date** – The date the payment was made to the vendor.
          This defaults to today's date but can be changed.

          5. **Memo** – An optional internal note for reference, such as a
          payment remark. This is auto-populated from the Purchase Order number
          by default.

          ##### Step 5 — Click Validate

          After reviewing all the payment details, click the **Validate** button
          to confirm and post the payment.

          The **Purchase Analysis** report gives you a visual and data-driven
          overview of all your purchase activities. It helps you monitor
          spending trends, track order volumes, and evaluate supplier

          ##### Step 1 — Go to Purchase Analysis

          From the top navigation menu, click **Reporting** ‣ **Purchase
          Analysis**.

          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/0001.png)

          ##### Step 2 — Understand the Report Views

          The Purchase Analysis report offers multiple view modes so you can
          analyze your data in different ways. Use the view toggle icons at the
          top-left of the report to switch between views:

          #### Line Chart View

          Displays purchase data as a continuous line or area chart over time.
          This is useful for tracking purchase trends across a date range, such
          as daily or monthly spending patterns.


          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/0002.png)

          #### Bar Chart View

          Displays purchase data as vertical bars grouped by a selected
          dimension such as Confirmation Date, Vendor, or Product. This makes it
          easy to compare purchase volumes or totals between groups at a glance.


          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/0003.png)

          #### Pie Chart View

          Displays purchase data as a proportional pie chart. This is ideal for
          understanding the share or percentage contribution of each group

          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/0004.png)

          #### Pivot Table View

          Displays purchase data in a structured table format with rows,
          columns, and subtotals. This is the most detailed view and is useful
          for in-depth data analysis and exporting reports.

          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/0005.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Tip" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            You can switch between the **Pivot Table** and **Chart** views at any time using the view toggle icons on the right side of the toolbar. Each view retains your current filters and groupings.
          </Noted>

          Click the **Measures** button at the top-left to select which data
          columns or metrics you want to display in the report. You can enable
          or disable any combination of the following measures:


          1. **# of Lines** – The total number of purchase order lines included
          in the analysis.

          2. **Average Cost** – The average unit cost across all purchase lines.

          3. **Days to Confirm** – The average number of days taken to confirm a
          purchase order from its creation date.

          4. **Days to Receive** – The average number of days taken to receive
          goods after the purchase order was confirmed.

          5. **Gross Weight** – The total gross weight of all purchased

          6. **Qty Billed** – The total quantity of items that have been billed.

          7. **Qty Ordered** – The total quantity of items ordered across all

          8. **Qty Received** – The total quantity of items physically received

          9. **Qty to be Billed** – The quantity of received items that have not
          yet been billed.

          10. **Total** – The grand total value of all purchase orders including
          taxes.

          11. **Untaxed Total** – The total value of all purchase orders

          12. **Volume** – The total volume of purchased products.

          13. **Count** – The total number of purchase order records included in

        body: |
          The **Purchase Register** report provides a detailed line-by-line
          record of all purchase transactions. Unlike the Purchase Analysis
          which focuses on trends and aggregates, the Purchase Register shows
          each individual order line with its full transaction details — making
          it ideal for auditing, reconciliation, and financial reporting.

          ##### Step 1 — Go to Purchase Register

          From the top navigation menu, click **Reporting** ‣ **Purchase
          Register**.

          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/register/0001.png)

          ##### Step 2 — Understand the Report Views

          The Purchase Register offers two view modes you can switch between
          using the view toggle icons at the top of the report:

          #### List View

          Displays all purchase order lines in a flat, row-by-row format. Each
          row represents a single order line and shows the following columns:

          1. **Date** – The date the purchase order was created or confirmed.

          2. **Order Number** – The unique purchase order number (e.g., P00008,
          P00009).

          3. **Vendor** – The supplier name associated with the order line.

          4. **Product** – The product or service purchased on that line.

          5. **UOM** – The unit of measure for the product (e.g., Units, Can,
          kg).

          6. **Quantity** – The number of units ordered on that line.

          7. **Price** – The unit price of the product.

          8. **Tax** – The tax amount applied to that line.

          9. **Amount** – The total amount for that line including taxes.

          The bottom row displays the **grand totals** for Quantity, Price
          (weighted), Tax, and Amount across all lines.

          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/register/0002.png)

          #### Pivot Table View

          Displays the purchase register data in a structured pivot table
          grouped by product and period (e.g., January 2026, February 2026).
          This view is useful for comparing purchase volumes and amounts across
          different time periods side by side.

          Each row shows a product with its **Price**, **Quantity**, and
          **Amount** broken down by month, with a **Total** column summarizing
          all periods.

          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/register/0003.png)

          ##### Step 3 — Customize Measures (Pivot View)

          When in **Pivot Table View**, click the **Measures** button to select
          which columns are displayed. The available measures for the Purchase
          Register are:

          1. **Amount** *(Active by default)* – The total amount for each line
          including taxes.

          2. **Base UOM QTY** – The quantity expressed in the product's base
          unit of measure.

          3. **Billed** – The quantity or amount that has been billed to date.

          4. **Price** *(Active by default)* – The unit price of the product on
          each order line.

          5. **Product QTY** – The product quantity as ordered.

          6. **Quantity** *(Active by default)* – The number of units ordered.

          7. **Received** – The quantity of goods received against the order
          line.

          8. **Tax** – The tax amount applied to each line.

          9. **Count** – The number of order line records included in the
          report.

          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/register/0004.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Tip" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            Active measures are shown with a checkmark. Tick or untick any measure to add or remove it from the current pivot table view.
          </Noted>


          ##### Step 4 — Filter the Data

          Click the **Filters** button to narrow down the records displayed in
          the report. The available filter options are:

          1. **RFQs** – Show only records with a status of Request for
          Quotation.

          2. **Purchase Orders** – Show only confirmed Purchase Orders.

          3. **Cancelled** – Show only cancelled purchase records.

          4. **Fully Billed** – Show only order lines that have been fully
          billed.

          5. **Fully Received** – Show only order lines where goods have been
          fully received.

          6. **Add Custom Filter** – Define your own filter conditions for more
          specific queries.

          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/register/0005.png)

          ##### Step 5 — Group the Data

          Click the **Group By** button to organize the report records into
          logical groups. The available grouping options are:


          1. **Products** – Group all lines by product name.

          2. **Vendor** – Group all lines by supplier.

          3. **Category** – Group all lines by product category.

          4. **Date** – Group records by order date (with sub-options for day,
          week, month, quarter, or year).

          5. **Receipt Date** – Group records by the date goods were received
          (with sub-options for period).

          6. **Add Custom Group** – Define your own grouping field for more
          specific analysis.

          ![](/img/purchase/reporting/register/0006.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Tip" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            You can combine multiple **Group By** options and **Filters** together to create highly targeted views. For example, grouping by **Vendor** and filtering by **Purchase Orders** gives you a breakdown of confirmed purchases per supplier. Use the **Export** button in List View to download the report as a spreadsheet for further analysis.
          </Noted>
