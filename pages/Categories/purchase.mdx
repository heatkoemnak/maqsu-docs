      A **Request for Quotation (RFQ)** is the first supplier document in the purchase flow. Use it when you need to request prices, quantities, or order details from a vendor before confirming a purchase order.

          Use the following steps to create a request for quotation:

          ##### Step 1 — Navigation

          From the left sidebar, click on **Purchase** to open the Purchase
          module. Then, from the top navigation menu, go to **Orders** ‣
          **Requests for Quotation**.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0001.png)

          Once you navigate to **Requests for Quotation**, you will see the
          master list of all existing RFQs. The list displays the following
          columns:
          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0002.png)

          1. **RFQ Number** – The unique document number automatically assigned
          to each request (e.g., P00008, P00009).

          2. **Order Date** – The date and time the RFQ was created.

          3. **Vendor** – The name of the supplier associated with the request.

          4. **Reference** – An optional reference identifier for internal
          tracking.

          5. **Purchase Representative** – The MAQSU user responsible for managing this request.

          6. **Total** – The total value of the RFQ.

          7. **Status** – The current status of the document, such as **RFQ** or
          **Purchase Order**.

          <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
            You can use the **Filters**, **Group By**, and **Search** bar at the top right to quickly find specific records. You can also switch between **List**, **Kanban**, and **Calendar** views using the view icons.
          </Noted>

          ##### Step 2 — Create

          To start a new purchase request, click the **Create** button at the
          top-left of the Requests for Quotation list page.


          This will open a blank **Request for Quotation** form ready for you to
          fill in.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0003.png)

          ##### Step 3 — Complete the Fields

          Once the form is open in **New** status, complete the following fields:

          1. **Vendor** *(Required)* – Search and select the supplier by name,
          TIN, email, or reference. This field identifies who you are sending
          the quotation request to. Start typing and select the correct vendor
          from the dropdown list.

          2. **Reference** *(Optional)* – Enter an internal reference number or
          note to help identify this request. This is useful for linking the RFQ
          to an internal purchase requisition or project.

          3. **Doc. No.** *(Auto)* – This field is automatically generated by the system.
          You can fill this in manually if you have your own document number.

          4. **Order Date** *(Auto)* – This is automatically set to the current date and
          time. You can adjust it if needed.

          5. **Currency** *(Auto)* – The currency used for this purchase request.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0004.png)

          Under the **Order Lines** tab, add the products or services you wish
          to request:

          * Click **Add a product** to add a new line item.

          * Fill in the **Product**, **Description**, **Quantity**, **UOM**,
          **Unit Price**, **Discount**, and **Taxes** for each line.

          * The **Subtotal** will be calculated automatically.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0005.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Tip" image="/img/icon/tips.png">
            You can also click **Add a section** to group your order lines, or **Add a note** to include internal remarks on a specific line.
          </Noted>

          ##### Step 4 — Save

          After filling in all the required fields, click the **Save** button at
          the top-left to save your new Purchase Request for Quotation. Once saved, the system will assign a **Doc. No.** automatically and
          the RFQ will appear in the master list with the status **RFQ**.

          ![](/img/purchase/requests/0006.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Next step" image="/img/icon/next.png">
            After saving, you can send the RFQ to your supplier by clicking **Send by Email**, or confirm it directly into a **Purchase Order** by clicking **Confirm Order**.
          </Noted>

        A **Purchase Order** is a formal document that confirms the purchase of products or services from a supplier. It serves as a legally binding agreement between the buyer and the vendor, outlining the details of the purchase such as quantities, prices, delivery dates, and terms of payment.

        In MAQSU ERP, you can create a Purchase Order by either confirming an existing Request for Quotation (RFQ) or by creating a new one directly from scratch. Both methods will lead you to the same Purchase Order form where you can manage your purchase details.

    body: |
      Receiving goods is a critical step in the purchasing process in MAQSU ERP.
      It confirms that the ordered products have been physically delivered by
      the supplier, updates your inventory stock levels, and prepares the
      Purchase Order for vendor billing.


      <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
        Always verify that the quantities and products received match the Purchase Order
        before validating the goods receipt. Any discrepancies should be resolved with the
        supplier before completing the process.
      </Noted>

      Once a Purchase Order has been confirmed, the next step is to receive
      the goods from the supplier. Follow the steps below to complete the
      goods receiving process in MAQSU ERP.

      ---

      ##### Step 1 — Navigattion

      From the left sidebar, click **Purchase**. Then from the top
      navigation menu, go to **Orders** ‣ **Purchase Orders**.

      ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0001.png)

      ---

      ##### Step 2 — Find the Order

      Locate the Purchase Order you want to receive goods for by utilizing
      the **Filters** feature. Click the **Filters** button at the top-right
      of the list, then select **Billing Status is "Nothing to Bill"** from the
      filter options to narrow down the results.

      ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0002.png)

      <Noted type="info" title="Filters"
      image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
        If **Billing Status is "Nothing to Bill"** does not appear in the standard filter list, you can use a **Custom Filter** instead. Click **Add Custom Filter**, then set the conditions as follows:

        1. **Field:** Billing Status
        2. **Operator:** is
        3. **Value:** Nothing to Bill

        Then click **Apply** to apply the custom filter.
      </Noted>

      After applying the filter, the list will show only Purchase Orders
      with a **Billing Status** of **Nothing to Bill**.

      ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0003.png)

      ---

      ##### Step 3 — Open Order

      Click on the **Order Number** of the Purchase Order you want to
      receive goods for to open it.

      You will see the Purchase Order detail page showing the vendor, order
      date, and the **Order Lines** with the ordered products, quantities,
      and amounts. Note that the **Received** column will show **0.00**,
      indicating that no goods have been received yet.

      ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0004.png)

      ---

      ##### Step 4 — Receive Products

      Click the **Receive Products** button at the top of the Purchase Order
      form to start the goods receiving process.

      This will open the **Goods Receipt** form (e.g., WH/IN/00004) which
      shows all products from the Purchase Order that are pending receipt.

      ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0005.png)

      <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
        The status bar at the top right shows the current stage of the receipt: **Draft → Waiting → Ready → Done**. When you open the receipt, it will be in **Ready** status, meaning the goods are ready to be validated.
      </Noted>

      ---

      ##### Step 5 — Validate

      After reviewing the goods receipt, click the **Validate** button to
      confirm that the goods have been physically received.

      ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0006.png)

      The Goods Receipt form displays:

      * **Receive From** – The vendor supplying the goods.

      * **Operation Type** – The warehouse operation type, typically **Goods
      Receipts**.

      * **Scheduled Date** – The expected date of receipt.

      * **Source Document** – The Purchase Order number this receipt is
      linked to.

      * **Operations Tab** – Lists each product with its **Demand** (ordered
      quantity) and **Done** (received quantity).

      ---

      ##### Step 6 — Apply

      A confirmation dialog — **Immediate Transfer?** — will appear. This
      dialog informs you that you have not yet recorded the **Done**
      quantities manually, and that clicking **Apply** will instruct MAQSU
      to automatically process all quantities as received.

      Click **Apply** to confirm and complete the goods receipt.

      ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0008.png)

      <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
        If you want to record partial receipts or specify exact done quantities manually before validating, click **Cancel** on this dialog, update the **Done** column for each product, then click **Validate** again.
      </Noted>

      ---

      ##### Step 7 — Verify

      After clicking **Apply**, the goods receipt status will automatically
      update to **Done**. You will also notice that:

      * The **Done** column now shows the received quantities matching the
      **Demand**.

      * An **Effective Date** has been recorded, showing the exact date and
      time the goods were received.

      * The available action buttons change to **Return**, **Scrap**, and
      **Unlock**, confirming the receipt is complete.

      ![](/img/purchase/receiving/0009.png)

      <Noted type="info" title="Next Step" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
        Once goods are received, the next step is to **Create a Bill** to record the supplier invoice and process payment against the Purchase Order.
      </Noted>

    body: |
        Creating a **Vendor Bill** in MAQSU ERP records the supplier's invoice
        into your accounting system after goods have been received. It ensures
        that your financial records accurately reflect what is owed to the
        supplier and prepares the transaction for payment.


        <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
          A vendor bill can only be created after the goods have been
          received and the Purchase Order billing status is **Waiting Bills**.
          Always verify the invoice details match the Purchase Order before posting
          the bill.
        </Noted>

        After goods have been received, the next step is to create a **Vendor
          Bill** to record the supplier invoice and process payment. Follow the
          steps below:

          ---

          ##### Step 1 — Navigation

          From the left sidebar, click **Purchase**. Then from the top
          navigation menu, go to **Orders** ‣ **Purchase Orders**.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0001.png)

          ---

          ##### Step 2 — Find the Order

          To locate the Purchase Order that is ready for billing, utilize the
          **Filters** feature to efficiently narrow down the list.

          Click the **Filters** button at the top-right of the list, and then select
          **Billing Status is "Waiting Bills"** from the filter options to retrieve only
          Purchase Orders with a billing status of **Waiting Bills**.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0002.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Can't find the filter?"
          image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            If **Billing Status is "Waiting Bills"** does not appear in the standard filter list, use a **Custom Filter** instead. Click **Add Custom Filter**, then set the conditions as follows:

            1. **Field:** Billing Status
            2. **Operator:** is
            3. **Value:** Waiting Bills

            Then click **Apply** to apply the custom filter.
          </Noted>

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0003.png)

          After applying the filter, the list will display only Purchase Orders
          with a **Billing Status** of **Waiting Bills**.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0004.png)

          ---

          ##### Step 3 — Open the Order

          Click on the **Order Number** of the Purchase Order you want to create
          a vendor bill for to open it.

          On the Purchase Order detail page, verify that goods have been
          received by checking the **Received** column in the Order Lines — it
          should now show the received quantity (e.g., 100.00). The **Billed**
          column will still show **0.00**, indicating no bill has been created
          yet.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0005.png)

          ---

          ##### Step 4 — Create Bill

          Click the **Create Bill** button at the top of the Purchase Order form
          to start the billing process.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0006.png)

          ---

          ##### Step 5 — Payment Type

          A **Create Bill** dialog will appear. The bill will be created in
          **Draft** status so you can review it before posting. Select the
          appropriate bill type:

          1. **Regular Bill** *(Default)* – Creates a standard vendor bill for
          the full amount of the purchase order. Use this for normal purchases.

          2. **Down Payment (Percentage)** – Creates a partial bill based on a
          percentage of the total purchase order amount. Use this when paying a
          deposit upfront.

          3. **Down Payment (Fixed Amount)** – Creates a partial bill for a
          specific fixed amount. Use this when the advance payment is a set
          amount rather than a percentage.

          Select the appropriate option, then click **Create and View Bill** to
          create the bill and open it immediately.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0007.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            If you click **Create Bill** instead of **Create and View Bill**, the bill will be created in the background without opening it. You can find it later under the **Accounting** module → **Vendor Bills**.
          </Noted>

          ---

          ##### Step 6 — Review the Draft Bill and Click Post

          The **Draft Bill** form will open automatically. Review all the
          details carefully before posting:

          1. **Vendor** – The supplier linked to this bill.

          2. **Bill Date** – The date the bill was created.

          3. **Accounting Date** – The date used for accounting entries.

          4. **Payment Terms** – The agreed payment due date.

          5. **Journal** – The accounting journal, typically **Vendor Bills
          (USD)**.

          6. **Invoice Lines** – Lists all products from the Purchase Order with
          their quantities, unit prices, accounts, and subtotals.


          Once you have verified all the information is correct, click the
          **Post** button to validate and post the vendor bill. This will change
          the status from **Draft** to **Posted** and record the corresponding
          journal entries in your accounting.

          ![](/img/purchase/vendor-bill/0008.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Next Step" image="/img/icon/sparkles.png">
            Once the vendor bill is posted, the next step is to **Register Payment** to record the actual payment made to the supplier against this bill.
          </Noted>
