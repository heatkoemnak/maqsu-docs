          body: |
                **e-Filing** feature simplifies tax compliance by generating government-mandated tax reports directly from your accounting data. This ensures accurate, timely, and error-free submissions to tax authorities, reducing the risk of penalties and audits.

                - [Sales](/accounting/compliance#compliance-e-filing-reports-sales) – VAT collected from customers on sales transactions
                - [Purchase](/accounting/compliance#compliance-e-filing-reports-purchase) – VAT paid to suppliers on purchases
                - [Withholding](/accounting/compliance#compliance-e-filing-reports-withholdingtax) – Tax withheld from vendor payments and employee compensation
                - [Monthly Tax Payment](/accounting/compliance#compliance-e-filing-reports-monthlytaxpayment) – Summary of tax obligations for the period


                <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
                    e-Filing reports must be generated and submitted to tax authorities according to the official filing schedule. Ensure all invoices and bills for the reporting period are posted before generating reports.
                </Noted>

                ***

                ### Sales

                Sales tax reports show all VAT collected from customers on sales transactions during the reporting period. These reports are required for monthly or quarterly VAT filing with tax authorities.

                ---

                ##### Step 1 — Configuration

                For more information on how to configure tax for sales reports, see the [Configuration Guide](/compliance/e-filing#e-filing-e-filing-setup-configuresalestax).

                ---

                ##### Step 2 — Navigation

                From the top navigation menu, click **Compliance**. Under the **E-Filing** section, select **Sales**.

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/sales/0001.png)

                ***

                ##### Step 3 — Choose Report Format

                The Sales report is available in two formats:

                ###### Web Format

                Click the **Web** tab to view the report in a browser-friendly format with detailed information:

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/sales/0002.png)

                The Web format displays all details in a readable table with bilingual headers (Khmer and English) for easy review before filing.

                ###### Desktop Format

                Click the **Desktop** tab to view the report in a simplified format optimized for export and e-filing:

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/sales/0003.png)

                The Desktop format uses a more compact layout that matches government e-filing system requirements.

                ***

                ##### Step 4 — Export the Report

                To export the report for submission to tax authorities:

                1. Click the **Print** dropdown button at the top-left of the report

                2. Select your preferred export format:
                    - **Sales (Desktop - Unformatted) XLSX** – Raw data export for further processing
                    - **Sales (Desktop) XLSX** – Formatted Excel file ready for e-filing
                    - **Sales (Web) XLSX** – Detailed web format in Excel

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/sales/0004.png)

                3. The report will be downloaded as an Excel file to your computer

                4. Open the Excel file to verify all data is correct

                5. Upload the file to the government e-filing portal according to their submission process

                ***

                <Noted type="warning" title="Important" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
                    Only include transactions with valid tax numbers in your e-filing reports. Verify that all
                    customer TIN/BIN/TID information is accurate before filing, as errors may result in rejected
                    submissions or penalties.
                </Noted>

                ***

                ### Monthly Tax Payment

                Monthly Tax Payment reports consolidate all tax obligations for the reporting period, including VAT, withholding tax, and other tax payments. These reports provide a comprehensive view of your total tax liability and help ensure timely payment to tax authorities.

                Before generating Monthly Tax Payment reports, you need to configure the tax settings that determine how taxes are calculated and tracked.

                ---

                ##### Step 1 — Configuration
                For more information on how to configure tax for sales reports, see the [Configuration Guide](/compliance/e-filing#e-filing-e-filing-setup-configuremonthlytaxpayment).

                ---

                ##### Step 2 — Navigation

                From the top navigation menu, click **Compliance**. Under the **E-Filing** section, select **Monthly Tax Payment**.

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/monthly-tax-payment/0001.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 3 — Review Report

                The Monthly Tax Payment report displays all tax obligations for the selected period in a comprehensive table format:

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/monthly-tax-payment/0002.png)

                The report shows:

                - **Tax Type** – Description of each tax obligation (e.g., Payment for Rental/Lease of Movable and Immovable Property, Payment of Dividend)
                - **Month** – The reporting period for each tax payment
                - **Rate** – The applicable tax rate percentage
                - **Tax Amount (USD)** – Tax liability in US Dollars
                - **Tax Amount (KHR)** – Tax liability in Khmer Riel
                - **Total Tax Payment** – Sum of all tax obligations for the period

                The report includes a **Summary** section at the bottom showing:

                - **VAT Carried Forward** – VAT credit from previous periods
                - **VAT Input** – Input VAT paid on purchases during the period
                - **VAT Import** – VAT paid on imported goods
                - **VAT Output** – Output VAT collected from sales
                - **VAT to be Paid** – Net VAT liability for the period
                - **VAT Carry Forward** – VAT credit to carry to next period

                ***

                ##### Step 4 — Export

                To export the report for submission to tax authorities or record-keeping:

                1. Click the **Print** dropdown button at the top-left of the report

                2. Select your preferred export format:

                    - **Monthly Tax Payment PDF** – Formatted PDF document for printing or digital submission
                    - **Monthly Tax Payment XLSX** – Standard Excel file with formatting
                    - **Monthly Tax Payment Detail XLSX** – Detailed Excel file with transaction-level information

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/monthly-tax-payment/0003.png)

                3. The report will be downloaded to your computer in the selected format

                4. Open the file to verify all data is correct

                5. Use the file for:


                    - Submission to the government e-filing portal
                    - Internal review and approval processes
                    - Accounting records and audit trail
                    - Payment processing and remittance


                ***

                <Noted type="warning" title="Important" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
                    Ensure all tax payments are remitted to tax authorities by the statutory deadline to avoid
                    penalties and interest charges. Review the Monthly Tax Payment report carefully before
                    submission to verify all amounts are accurate and complete.
                </Noted>

                To set up e-filing in MAQSU ERP, follow these steps:

                Additional configuration and setup guides to help you get the most out of MAQSU ERP's compliance features.

                ***

                ### Configure Sales Tax

                Set up the tax categories that will appear in your Sales e-filing reports.

                Now let's configure the tax categories for your Sales e-filing reports. This setup determines how different tax rates are grouped and reported in your sales tax filings.

                ---

                ##### Step 1 — Navigation

                Navigate to **Compliance** ‣ **E-Filing** ‣ **Sales**

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/config/sales/0001.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 2 — Click Configuration

                On the Sales report page, click the **Configuration** button. This will open the Sales Tax Configuration modal where you can review and assign tax rates to categories.

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/config/sales/0002.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 3 — Assign Taxes to Categories

                For each tax category, select which specific taxes should be included:

                1. Click on the **Taxes** dropdown for a category (e.g., "VAT on Sale")

                2. A searchable dropdown will appear showing all available taxes in your system.

                3. Select one or multiple taxes to assign to the category

                4. Selected taxes will appear as tags in the field

                5. Use the **Search More...** option to find additional taxes

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/config/sales/0004.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 4 — Save

                Once you have assigned the appropriate taxes to each category, click the **Save** button to apply your configuration. This will ensure that when you generate your Sales e-filing report, transactions with the assigned tax rates will be grouped under the correct categories.

                <Noted type="info" title="Tip" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
                    You can assign multiple tax rates to a single category. For example, "VAT on Sale" might
                    include both "VAT - Sales of Goods 10%" and "VAT - Sales of Services 10%" to consolidate
                    similar taxes in your report.
                </Noted>

                ---

                ### Configure Purchase Tax

                Set up the tax categories for your Purchase e-filing reports.

                ---

                ##### Step 1 — Navigation

                Navigate to **Compliance** ‣ **E-Filing** ‣ **Purchase**

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/config/purchase/0001.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 2 — Click Configuration

                Click the **Configuration** button in the options area to open the Purchase Tax Configuration modal.

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/config/purchase/0002.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 3 — Assign Taxes to Categories

                1. Click the **Taxes** dropdown for each category
                2. Select from available purchase-related taxes.
                3. Assign appropriate taxes to each category based on your business needs

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/config/purchase/0003.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 3 — Save

                After assigning taxes to all categories, click the **Save** button to apply your configuration. This will ensure that when you generate your Purchase e-filing report, transactions with the assigned tax rates will be grouped under the correct categories.

                <Noted type="warning" title="Important" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
                    Ensure "Local Taxable Purchase" includes only creditable VAT. Non-creditable purchases should
                    be assigned to the "Non Creditable Purchase" category to maintain accurate input VAT
                    calculations.
                </Noted>

                ***

                Set up withholding tax categories and their corresponding accounts.

                ---

                ##### Step 1 — Navigation

                Navigate to **Compliance** ‣ **E-Filing** ‣ **Withholding Tax**

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/config/witholding/0001.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 1 — Click Configuration

                Click the **Configuration** button to open the Withholding Tax Configuration modal where you can review and assign accounts to each withholding tax category.

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/config/witholding/0002.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 2 — Assign Taxes to Category

                For each withholding tax category, select the corresponding tax accounts:

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/config/witholding/0003.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 3 — Save

                After assigning accounts to all withholding tax categories, click the **Save** button to apply your configuration. This will ensure that when you generate your Withholding Tax e-filing report, transactions with the assigned tax rates will be grouped under the correct categories.

                </Noted>

                ### Configure Monthly Tax Payment

                Set up the tax accounts and parameters that determine how your Monthly Tax Payment report is generated.

                ---

                ##### Step 1 — Navigation

                Navigate to Monthly Tax Payment Reports by clicking **Compliance** from the top navigation menu. Under the **E-Filing** section, select **Monthly Tax Payment**.

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/monthly-tax-payment/0001.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 2 — Click on Configuration

                This will show you the Tax Configuration Dialog Settings

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/monthly-tax-payment/0006.png)

                ---

                ##### Step 3 — Configure Accounts

                In the Tax Configuration dialog, configure the following general settings:

                1. **Carry Forward Account** – Select the account for tracking VAT credits carried forward from previous periods (e.g., 106200 VAT Credit Carry Forward)

                2. **Tax On Salary** – Select the account for salary tax payable (e.g., 202100 Salary Tax Payable)

                3. **Fringe Tax** – Select the account for fringe benefit tax payable (e.g., 202200 Fringe Benefit Tax Payable)

                4. **Salary Journal** – Select the journal used for salary transactions (e.g., Miscellaneous Operations (USD))

                ![](/img/accounting/compliance/e-filing/monthly-tax-payment/0007.png)

                Each tax label includes:

                - **Label** – The tax type description
                - **Rate** – The withholding tax percentage
                - **Display On Zero** – Option to show/hide taxes with zero amounts in reports
                - **Note** – Additional information or comments

                ---

                ##### Step 4 — Save

                After configuring all settings, click the **Save** button at the bottom of the dialog to save your configuration

                <Noted type="info" title="Configuration Note" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
                    Tax configuration settings apply system-wide. Any changes made here will affect all Monthly
                    Tax Payment reports going forward. Ensure all settings are correct before saving.
                </Noted>
