      The **Goods Receipts** process is a critical component of inventory management, allowing you to track and document the arrival of stock from your vendors. This ensures that your physical inventory levels match your digital records and provides a clear audit trail for every item entering your warehouse.

      > **Note:** Goods Receipts remain in the "Ready" status until the physical items are counted and the transaction is validated. Once validated, the inventory levels are updated, and the record moves to "Done."

      ---

      ### Access Goods Receipts


      From the left sidebar, click **Inventory**. On the Inventory Overview dashboard, locate the **Goods Receipts** card (marked by the **Red Arrow**) and click on it to view all incoming shipments.


      ![Accessing Goods Receipts from the Inventory Dashboard](/img/inventory/goods-receipts/0001.png)

      ---

      ### Goods Receipts Overview

      The list view displays all receipt operations, categorized by their current progress. You can quickly identify the status of any shipment using the **Status** column:

      1.  **Ready** – Items that are expected and available to be processed.
      2.  **Done** – Receipts that have been fully processed and added to stock.
      3.  **Cancelled** – Orders that were terminated before completion.

      ![Overview of the Goods Receipts list](/img/inventory/goods-receipts/0002.png)

      ---

      ### Creating a New Receipt

      If you need to record an incoming shipment manually without a pre-existing purchase order:

      1.  Click the **Create** button (marked by the **Red Arrow**) at the top left.
      2.  Fill in the vendor details in the **Receive From** field and set the **Scheduled Date**.

      ![Creating a new Goods Receipt](/img/inventory/goods-receipts/0003.png)

      ---

      ### Processing a Receipt (WH/IN/00028)

      To receive items into your warehouse, select a specific receipt (e.g., **WH/IN/00028**) from the list to open the detailed view.

      1.  **Review Products**: Check the **Operations** tab to see the items and their **Demand** quantities.
      2.  **Edit Quantities**: If the delivered amount differs from the demand, click the line to adjust the **Done** quantity.
      3.  **Save Changes**: Click the **Save** button (marked by the **Red Arrow**) to store your updates.

      ![Reviewing and saving the receipt details](/img/inventory/goods-receipts/0004.png)

      ![Saving the record](/img/inventory/goods-receipts/0005.png)

      ---

      ### Validating the Receipt

      Once you have confirmed that the physical items match the record, you must finalize the transaction:

      1.  Click the **Validate** button (marked by the **Red Arrow**).
      2.  The system will prompt you if there is a quantity mismatch (e.g., creating a Backorder).
      3.  Upon validation, the status indicator in the top right will change to **Done**.

      ![Validating the receipt to update inventory](/img/inventory/goods-receipts/0006.png)

      ---

      ### Completed Receipt Status

      Once the process is finished, the receipt is locked. The **Effective Date** is recorded, and the items are officially added to your **WH/Stock** location.

      ![Finalized Goods Receipt in Done status](/img/inventory/goods-receipts/0007.png)

      <Noted type="info" title="Pro Tip" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
      Always compare the "Source Document" (e.g., NPO-260005) against the vendor's physical packing slip before clicking Validate to ensure 100% inventory accuracy.
      </Noted>

      ---

      For details on moving items between internal warehouse locations, see the [Internal Transfers Guide](/inventory/internal-transfers).

          A **Delivery Order** is a document that authorizes and records the shipment of goods from a warehouse or source location to a customer or destination. In MAQSU ERP, delivery orders are essential for managing outbound inventory movements, tracking shipments, and ensuring accurate fulfillment of sales orders.

          > **Note:** Delivery orders are typically created after confirming a sales order or quotation. They serve as the link between order management and inventory, ensuring products are properly allocated and shipped to customers. Accurate delivery processing is vital for customer satisfaction and inventory control.

          ---

          ### Step 1: Go to Delivery Orders

          From the left sidebar, click **Inventory**. Then from the Dashboard, locate and click on the **Delivery Orders** card in the inventory operations section.

          ![](/img/inventory/delivery-orders/0001.png)

          ---

          ### Step 2: Delivery Orders List Overview

          Once you navigate to **Delivery Orders**, you will see the master list of all existing delivery orders. The list displays the following columns:

          1. **Reference** – The unique delivery order reference number (e.g., WH/OUT/00022).
          2. **From** – The source location from which goods are being shipped (e.g., WH/Stock).
          3. **To** – The destination location where goods are being delivered (e.g., Partner Locations/Customers).
          4. **Contact** – The name of the customer or contact person receiving the delivery.
          5. **Scheduled Date** – The planned date and time for the delivery.
          6. **Source Document** – The originating document reference (e.g., sales order number Q-0003).
          7. **Batch Transfer** – Any batch or serial number tracking associated with the delivery.
          8. **Status** – The current document status: **Draft**, **Waiting**, **Ready**, **Done**, or **Cancelled**.

          The list view also shows color-coded status indicators:
          - **Done** (black text) – Delivery has been completed and validated
          - **Ready** (red text) – Products are available and ready for shipment
          - **Cancelled** (gray text) – Delivery order has been cancelled

          ![](/img/inventory/delivery-orders/0002.png)

          ---

          ### Step 3: Click Create

          Click the **Create** button at the top-left of the Delivery Orders list page to open a new blank delivery order form.

          ![](/img/inventory/delivery-orders/0003.png)

          ---

          ### Step 4: Fill Out the Delivery Order Form

          The new delivery order form will open in **Draft** status. Fill in the following required and optional fields:

          #### Header Fields

          1. **Delivery Address** *(Required)* – Search and select the customer or delivery address. Start typing the customer name and select the correct entry from the dropdown list. This field determines where the goods will be shipped.

          2. **Operation Type** *(Required)* – This is automatically set based on the delivery operation type (e.g., "My Company: Delivery Orders"). This determines the workflow and inventory locations used.

          3. **Source Location** *(Required)* – The warehouse location from which products will be picked and shipped. This typically defaults to your main stock location (e.g., "WH/Stock").

          4. **Doc. No.** – Automatically generated by the system after validation. You do not need to fill this in manually. It will appear as "Auto Numbering" initially.

          5. **Scheduled Date** *(Required)* – The planned date and time when the delivery should occur. This helps coordinate logistics and customer expectations.

          6. **Inventory Date** – The accounting date for inventory movements. This determines when the stock changes are recorded in your system.

          7. **Source Document** *(Optional)* – The reference to the originating sales order, quotation, or other document that created this delivery order. This helps trace the delivery back to its source.

          ![](/img/inventory/delivery-orders/0004.png)

          Once you fill in the delivery address and other header details, the form will update to show the selected information.

          ---

          ### Step 5: Add Products to Ship

          Under the **Detailed Operations** tab, add the products that need to be delivered to the customer. Click **Add a line** to add a new product line, then fill in the following fields for each item:

          1. **Product** *(Required)* – Choose the product being delivered. Start typing the product name or code and select the correct entry. The system will auto-populate related fields.

          2. **From** – The specific source location from which this product will be picked. This is typically the same as the main source location specified in the header.

          3. **Reserved** – The quantity that has been reserved or allocated for this delivery from your inventory. This shows what is committed to this shipment.

          4. **Done** – The actual quantity that has been picked and prepared for shipment. Initially, this will be 0.00 and should be updated when products are physically picked.

          5. **UOM** – The unit of measure for the product (e.g., Pcs, Units, kg, liters). This should match the product's standard UOM.

          The system tracks both reserved quantities (what should be shipped) and done quantities (what has actually been prepared).

          ![](/img/inventory/delivery-orders/0005.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
          Carefully verify that all quantities are correct before validating the delivery. The "Reserved" quantity shows what should be shipped, while the "Done" quantity tracks what has actually been picked and prepared.
          </Noted>

          ---

          ### Step 6: Save the Delivery Order

          After completing all required fields and adding product lines, click **Save** to save the delivery order. At this stage, the delivery remains in **Draft** status and can still be edited.

          ![](/img/inventory/delivery-orders/0006.png)

          ---

          ### Step 7: Click Validate to Confirm Availability

          Once you have reviewed the delivery order, click the **Validate** button to check product availability and reserve the quantities. This will:

          - Change the status from **Draft** to **Waiting** or **Ready**
          - Reserve the specified quantities from inventory
          - Confirm that products are available for shipment

          The status will change to:
          - **Waiting** – If products are not yet available or reserved
          - **Ready** – If all products are available and ready to ship

          ![](/img/inventory/delivery-orders/0007.png)

          After validation, the delivery order cannot be edited unless you unreserve it first. The **Done** column becomes editable, allowing you to track the actual picked quantities.

          ---

          ### Step 8: Update Done Quantities and Complete Delivery

          When products are physically picked and ready to ship, update the **Done** quantities to match what has been prepared:

          1. In the **Detailed Operations** tab, update the **Done** column for each product line to reflect the actual quantity picked and packed.

          2. The **Done** quantity should typically match the **Reserved** quantity, unless there are shortages or partial shipments.

          3. Once all done quantities are entered, click the status button (currently showing **Ready**) to advance the delivery order to **Done** status.

          ![](/img/inventory/delivery-orders/0008.png)

          When you mark the delivery as **Done**:
          - The inventory quantities are deducted from your stock
          - The delivery order is locked and cannot be edited
          - The shipment is officially recorded in your system
          - Inventory movements are posted to your accounting records

          <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
          If you need to modify a completed delivery order, you may need to create a return or adjustment. Contact your system administrator for guidance on handling delivery corrections or returns.
          </Noted>

          ---

          ### Additional Operations and Features

          #### Partial Deliveries

          If you cannot ship the full reserved quantity, you can update the **Done** quantities to reflect a partial shipment. The remaining quantities can be handled in several ways:
          - Create a backorder for the remaining items
          - Cancel the remaining quantity if no longer needed
          - Adjust the original sales order to reflect the partial fulfillment

          #### Batch and Serial Number Tracking

          For products that require batch or serial number tracking, additional fields will appear in the **Detailed Operations** tab. You must specify which batches or serial numbers are being shipped to maintain proper traceability.

          #### Printing Delivery Documents

          After validating the delivery order, you can print delivery documents for the shipping team or customer:
          1. Click the **Print** button at the top of the form
          2. Select the appropriate delivery document template
          3. Generate a PDF with all delivery details, products, and quantities

          #### Cancelling a Delivery Order

          If a delivery needs to be cancelled:
          1. Open the delivery order
          2. Click the **Cancel** button
          3. The status will change to **Cancelled**
          4. Reserved quantities will be released back to available inventory

          ---

          ### Delivery Order Workflow Summary

          1. **Draft** → Create delivery order and add products
          2. **Save** → Save the draft delivery order
          3. **Validate** → Reserve inventory and confirm availability (→ Waiting/Ready)
          4. **Update Done Quantities** → Record actual picked quantities
          5. **Mark as Done** → Complete the delivery and deduct inventory

          ---

          For more details on inventory management and delivery operations, see the [Inventory Management Guide](/inventory/inventory-overview).

          An **Internal Transfer** is a document that records the movement of products between different locations within the same company. In MAQSU ERP, internal transfers are essential for managing warehouse-to-warehouse movements, location reallocations, and inventory reorganization without affecting customer shipments or vendor receipts.

              > **Note:** Internal transfers are used to move stock between your own locations, such as from one warehouse to another, or from stock to a quality control area. These transfers maintain accurate inventory levels across all locations and ensure proper traceability of product movements.

              ---

              ### Step 1: Go to Internal Transfers

              From the left sidebar, click **Inventory**. Then from the Dashboard, locate and click on the **Internal Transfers** card in the inventory operations section.

              ![](/img/inventory/internal-transfers/0001.png)

              ---

              ### Step 2: Internal Transfers List Overview

              Once you navigate to **Internal Transfers**, you will see the list view. If no transfers have been created yet, you'll see an empty state with the message:

              **"Create a new transfer"**

              *"Transfers allow you to move products from one location to another."*

              This screen serves as the starting point for creating your first internal transfer.

              ![](/img/inventory/internal-transfers/0002.png)

              When transfers exist, the list will display the following columns:
              - **Reference** – The unique transfer reference number
              - **From** – The source location
              - **To** – The destination location
              - **Contact** – The responsible person or contact
              - **Scheduled Date** – The planned date for the transfer
              - **Source Document** – Any originating document reference
              - **Status** – Draft, Waiting, Ready, Done, or Cancelled

              ---

              ### Step 3: Click Create

              Click the **Create** button at the top-left of the Internal Transfers page to open a new blank transfer form.

              ![](/img/inventory/internal-transfers/0003.png)

              ---

              ### Step 4: Fill Out the Internal Transfer Form

              The new internal transfer form will open in **Draft** status. Fill in the following required and optional fields:

              #### Header Fields

              1. **Contact** *(Optional)* – Search and select the person or team responsible for this transfer. This could be a warehouse manager, logistics coordinator, or any contact who oversees the movement. Start typing and select from the dropdown list.

              2. **Operation Type** *(Required)* – This is automatically set to "My Company: Internal Transfers" and determines the workflow for the transfer operation.

              3. **Source Location** *(Required)* – Select the warehouse or location from which products will be moved. This is where the inventory will be deducted. For example, "WH/Stock" for your main warehouse stock area.

              4. **Destination Location** *(Required)* – Select the target warehouse or location where products will be moved to. This is where the inventory will be added. For example, "WH/Stock2" for a secondary warehouse or storage area.

              5. **Doc. No.** – Automatically generated by the system after validation. You do not need to fill this in manually. It will show "Auto Numbering" initially.

              6. **Scheduled Date** *(Required)* – The planned date and time when the transfer should occur. This helps coordinate logistics and warehouse operations.

              7. **Inventory Date** *(Optional)* – The accounting date for inventory movements. This determines when the stock changes are recorded in your financial system.

              8. **Source Document** *(Optional)* – An internal reference to any document that initiated this transfer, such as a purchase order, production order, or manual request number (e.g., P00032).

              ![](/img/inventory/internal-transfers/0004.png)

              <Noted type="info" title="Important" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
              The source and destination locations must be different. You cannot transfer products from a location to itself. Ensure you select the correct source and destination to maintain accurate inventory tracking across your warehouses.
              </Noted>

              ---

              ### Step 5: Add Products to Transfer

              Under the **Detailed Operations** tab, add the products that need to be moved between locations. The system may auto-populate products if this transfer was created from another document, or you can add them manually.

              For each product line, the following information is displayed:

              1. **Product** *(Required)* – The name and internal reference code of the product being transferred. The product name appears in red text to draw attention.

              2. **From** – The source location where the product will be picked from. This should match the Source Location specified in the header.

              3. **To** – The destination location where the product will be placed. This should match the Destination Location specified in the header.

              4. **Done** – The actual quantity that has been physically moved. Initially this will be 0.00 and should be updated during the transfer process.

              5. **UOM** – The unit of measure for the product (e.g., Pcs, Units, kg). This ensures consistency in quantity tracking.

              Click **Add a line** if you need to add additional products to the transfer.

              ![](/img/inventory/internal-transfers/0005.png)

              In the example shown, two products are being transferred:
              - **[1AB-438-001] ABB SN 564406 Z78/2301-002 (Second Hand)** – 3.00 Pcs
              - **[1AC-114-001] ACB-2500A (4P), LV with metal enclose (Complete Set)** – 3.00 Pcs

              Both are moving from WH/Stock to WH/Stock2.

              <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
              Verify that all product codes, quantities, and locations are correct before proceeding. Internal transfers affect inventory levels in both the source and destination locations, so accuracy is essential for proper stock management.
              </Noted>

              ---

              ### Step 6: Save the Internal Transfer

              After completing all required fields and adding product lines, click **Save** to save the internal transfer. At this stage, the transfer remains in **Draft** status and can still be edited or cancelled.

              The **Save** button is located at the top-left of the form.

              ---

              ### Step 7: Click Validate to Confirm the Transfer

              Once you have reviewed and confirmed all details are correct, click the **Validate** button to officially initiate the transfer. This will:

              - Change the transfer status from **Draft** to **Waiting** or **Ready**
              - Reserve the quantities from the source location
              - Generate the transfer operations in the system
              - Assign a permanent reference number (e.g., INT/00125)

              ![](/img/inventory/internal-transfers/0006.png)

              The status progression typically follows this pattern:
              - **Draft** → Initial state while editing
              - **Waiting** → Products reserved but not yet available
              - **Ready** → Products available and ready to be moved
              - **Done** → Transfer completed and inventory updated

              <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
              After validation, you can no longer freely edit the transfer. If you need to make changes, you may need to use the "Unreserve" button to return to Draft status, or create a new transfer for corrections.
              </Noted>

              ---

              ### Step 8: Update Done Quantities and Complete the Transfer

              When products are physically moved from the source to the destination location, update the **Done** quantities:

              1. In the **Detailed Operations** tab, update the **Done** column for each product line to reflect the actual quantity that has been moved.

              2. The **Done** quantity should match the requested quantity unless there are issues like damage, shortage, or partial movements.

              3. Once all done quantities are entered, the system will allow you to advance the transfer status.

              4. Click the appropriate status button or action to mark the transfer as complete.

              When you mark the transfer as **Done**:
              - Inventory is deducted from the source location
              - Inventory is added to the destination location
              - The transfer is locked and cannot be edited
              - All inventory movements are recorded in the system
              - Accounting entries are posted if configured

              ---

              ### Additional Operations and Features

              #### Partial Transfers

              If you cannot move the full quantity at once, you can update the **Done** quantities to reflect a partial transfer. The remaining quantities can be handled in several ways:
              - Create a backorder transfer for the remaining items
              - Cancel the remaining quantity if no longer needed
              - Complete multiple partial transfers until all quantities are moved

              #### Batch and Serial Number Tracking

              For products that require batch or serial number tracking, additional fields will appear allowing you to specify which specific batches or serial numbers are being transferred. This ensures complete traceability across locations.

              #### Transfer History and Traceability

              Each internal transfer is fully traceable in the system. You can view:
              - The complete transfer history for any product
              - Who created and validated the transfer
              - The exact date and time of inventory movements
              - Links to source and destination stock levels

              #### Cancelling a Transfer

              If a transfer needs to be cancelled before completion:
              1. Open the transfer document
              2. Click the **Cancel** button
              3. The status will change to **Cancelled**
              4. Reserved quantities will be released back to the source location
              5. No inventory movements will be recorded

              #### Scrapping During Transfer

              If products are damaged during the transfer process, you can use the **Scrap** button to remove them from inventory entirely rather than completing the transfer to the destination location.

              ---

              ### Internal Transfer Workflow Summary

              1. **Draft** → Create transfer and specify source/destination locations
              2. **Add Products** → Define which items and quantities to transfer
              3. **Save** → Save the draft transfer
              4. **Validate** → Reserve inventory and initiate the transfer (→ Waiting/Ready)
              5. **Update Done Quantities** → Record actual moved quantities
              6. **Mark as Done** → Complete the transfer and update inventory in both locations

              ---

              ### Best Practices for Internal Transfers

              - Always verify source and destination locations before validating
              - Use clear naming conventions for warehouse locations
              - Update done quantities promptly after physical movements
              - Use the Contact field to assign responsibility for the transfer
              - Link transfers to source documents for better traceability
              - Regularly review pending transfers to avoid inventory discrepancies
              - Conduct periodic physical counts to verify transfer accuracy

              ---

              For more details on inventory management and warehouse operations, see the [Inventory Management Guide](/inventory/inventory-overview).

          A **Batch Transfer** is a document that allows you to group multiple individual transfer operations together for efficient processing and management. In MAQSU ERP, batch transfers are essential for organizing related transfers, assigning jobs to specific personnel, and managing timing for multiple operations that need to be completed together.

              > **Note:** Batch transfers help improve operational efficiency by grouping transfers that should be done together, assigning them to specific team members, or scheduling them for coordinated execution. This is particularly useful for warehouse operations that involve multiple related movements or when managing workload distribution among staff.

              ---

              ### Step 1: Go to Batch Transfers

              From the left sidebar, click **Inventory**. From the Dashboard, hover over the **Transfers** menu and click on **Batch Transfers** from the dropdown menu.

              ![](/img/inventory/batch-transfers/0001.png)

              ---

              ### Step 2: Batch Transfers List Overview

              Once you navigate to **Batch Transfers**, you will see the list view. If no batch transfers have been created yet, you'll see an empty state with the message:

              **"Create a new batch transfer"**

              *"The goal of the batch transfer is to group operations that may (needs to) be done together in order to increase their efficiency. It may also be useful to assign jobs (one person = one batch) or help the timing management of operations (tasks to be done at 1pm)."*

              ![](/img/inventory/batch-transfers/0002.png)

              This screen provides context about the purpose of batch transfers and serves as the starting point for creating your first batch.

              When batch transfers exist, the list will display columns including:
              - **Reference** – The unique batch transfer identifier
              - **Responsible** – The person assigned to complete the batch
              - **Scheduled Date** – When the batch should be processed
              - **Status** – Draft, In Progress, or Done
              - **Transfer Count** – Number of transfers included in the batch

              ---

              ### Step 3: Create a New Batch Transfer

              Click the **Create** button at the top-left of the Batch Transfers page to open a new blank batch transfer form.

              ![](/img/inventory/batch-transfers/0003.png)

              ---

              ### Step 4: Fill Out the Batch Transfer Form

              The new batch transfer form will open in **Draft** status with a default name of "New". Fill in the following fields:

              #### Header Fields

              1. **Responsible** *(Optional)* – Select the person or team member who will be responsible for executing all transfers in this batch. This helps assign workload and accountability. For example, select "Administrator" or the specific warehouse staff member.

              The form initially shows minimal fields. The main work involves adding individual transfer operations to the batch in the **Transfers** tab.

              ![](/img/inventory/batch-transfers/0004.png)

              ---

              ### Step 5: Add Transfers to the Batch

              Under the **Transfers** tab, you need to add the individual transfer operations that should be grouped together in this batch. The tab displays a table with the following columns:

              - **Reference** – The transfer operation reference number
              - **Scheduled Date** – When each transfer is scheduled
              - **Source Location** – Where products are coming from
              - **Back Order of** – Any related backorder reference
              - **Source Document** – The originating document
              - **Status** – The current status of each transfer (Draft, Ready, etc.)

              #### Adding Transfers

              Click **Add a line** to open the transfer selection dialog.

              ![](/img/inventory/batch-transfers/0005.png)

            A **Adjustments** is a feature used to correct inventory quantities in the system to match the actual physical stock. In MAQSU, stock adjustments are essential for maintaining accurate inventory records by reconciling system quantities with physical counts, recording found or lost inventory, and correcting data entry errors.

            1. Conduct the physical count in the warehouse
            2. Locate each product in the Lines tab
            3. Enter the exact quantity counted in the **Counted** field
            4. The **Difference** column will automatically calculate
            5. Positive differences indicate found inventory (system was lower than reality)
            6. Negative differences indicate lost inventory (system was higher than reality)

            - Count systematically by location or product category
            - Use a second person to verify counts for high-value items
            - Take photos or notes for significant discrepancies
            - Count at a time when warehouse activity is minimal

            - Indicates lost stock, shrinkage, theft, or unrecorded shipments

            - Verify product identification (correct SKU)
            - Review recent movements in location
            - Consider recounting items with large differences
            - Document reasons for significant adjustments

            - The status changes from **In Progress** to **Validated**
            - Inventory quantities are updated in all locations
            - Stock movements are recorded for each adjustment
            - Accounting entries are posted (if configured)
            - The adjustment is locked and can no longer be edited
            - Inventory valuation is recalculated

            After validation, the adjustment is complete and your inventory records now match your physical count.

            3. **Start Inventory** → Generate product lines

            5. **Enter** → Input counted quantities in the Lines tab
            6. **Review** → Verify all differences and investigate discrepancies
            7. **Document** → Record reasons for significant adjustments
            8. **Validate** → Apply changes to inventory (→ Validated)

              ### Understanding Inventory Valuation

              #### How Valuation is Calculated

              The Total Value for each transaction depends on your inventory costing method:

              **FIFO (First In, First Out)**
              - Values stock based on the cost of the oldest inventory
              - When stock is sold/used, the cost of the oldest purchase is applied
              - Remaining inventory is valued at more recent purchase prices
              - Best for products where older inventory is sold first

              **AVCO (Average Cost)**
              - Calculates a weighted average cost of all inventory
              - Average cost is recalculated with each purchase
              - All units are valued at the current average cost
              - Best for interchangeable commodities or bulk materials

              **Standard Cost**
              - Uses a predetermined fixed cost per unit
              - Cost doesn't change unless manually updated
              - Variances between standard and actual are tracked separately
              - Best for manufacturing with stable material costs

              #### Why Some Values Show $0.00

              You may notice some transactions show a value of $0.00. This can occur when:

              1. **Products without cost data** – The product master doesn't have a cost configured
              2. **Inventory adjustments** – Some systems don't value adjustments until reconciliation
              3. **Zero-cost receipts** – Products received without a purchase price
              4. **Scrapped inventory** – Items removed from inventory with no salvage value
              5. **Configuration issues** – Costing method not properly set up

              <Noted type="warning" title="Important" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
              Zero-value transactions indicate missing cost data. For accurate financial reporting, ensure all products have proper cost information configured. Review and update product costs regularly, especially after receiving new purchases at different prices.
              </Noted>

              #### Transaction Types in the Report

              The Inventory Valuation report includes various transaction types:

              - **Receipts** – Goods received from suppliers (positive quantity, increases value)
              - **Deliveries** – Products shipped to customers (negative quantity, decreases value)
              - **Adjustments** – Inventory corrections from physical counts (positive or negative)
              - **Transfers** – Movements between locations (appears as decrease at source, increase at destination)
              - **Scrap** – Damaged or obsolete items removed (negative quantity)
              - **Production** – Finished goods from manufacturing (positive quantity)
              - **Consumption** – Materials used in production (negative quantity)

              ### Differences Between Inventory Reports

              #### Inventory Valuation vs. Inventory Balance

              **Inventory Valuation:**
              - Transaction-based view (one line per movement)
              - Shows historical changes over time
              - Includes positive and negative quantities
              - Organized chronologically by date
              - Focus on value changes and cost analysis
              - Used for financial reporting and cost accounting

              **Inventory Balance:**
              - Current status view (one line per product per location)
              - Shows only current on-hand quantities
              - Only positive quantities (what you have now)
              - Organized by product or location
              - Focus on stock availability and levels
              - Used for operations and inventory management

              #### When to Use Each Report

              **Use Inventory Valuation for:**
              - Month-end and year-end financial closing
              - Cost accounting and variance analysis
              - Audit documentation and compliance
              - Reconciliation with general ledger
              - Understanding value trends over time
              - Detailed transaction traceability

              **Use Inventory Balance for:**
              - Daily operational management
              - Stock availability checks
              - Reorder point monitoring
              - Physical count preparation
              - Quick inventory status overview
              - Location-based stock analysis
