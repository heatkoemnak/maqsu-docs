          A **Quotation** (also called a Sales Quote) is a formal document sent to a potential customer that outlines the products or services you offer, their quantities, unit prices, taxes, and the total amount. In MAQSU ERP, quotations are essential for initiating the sales process, managing customer negotiations, and converting opportunities into confirmed orders.

          > **Note:** Quotations are typically created at the beginning of the sales cycle before an order is confirmed. Once a customer accepts a quotation, it can be converted into a sales order. Accurate quotations help build customer trust and streamline the sales process.

          ---

          ### Step 1: Go to Quotations

          From the left sidebar, click **Sales**. Then from the top navigation menu, go to **Orders** → **Quotations**.

          ![](/img/sales/quote/0001.png)

          ---

          ### Step 2: Quotations List Overview

          Once you navigate to **Quotations**, you will see the master list of all existing sales quotations. The list displays the following columns:

          1. **Number** – The unique quotation number automatically assigned to each quote (e.g., S00001, S00002).
          2. **Reference** – An optional internal reference linked to the quotation.
          3. **Quotation Date** – The date the quotation was created.
          4. **Customer** – The name of the customer receiving the quotation.
          5. **Expiration Date** – The deadline by which the customer must accept the quote.
          6. **Total** – The total quotation amount including taxes.
          7. **Status** – The current document status: **Quotation**, **Quotation Sent**, **Sales Order**, or **Cancelled**.

          ![](/img/sales/quote/0002.png)

          ---

          ### Step 3: Click Create

          Click the **Create** button at the top-left of the Quotations list page to open a new blank quotation form.

          ![](/img/sales/quote/0003.png)

          ---

          ### Step 4: Fill Out the Quotation Form

          The new quotation form will open in **Quotation** status. Fill in the following required and optional fields:

          #### Header Fields

          1. **Customer** *(Required)* – Search and select the customer by name, code, email, or reference. Start typing and select the correct entry from the dropdown list.

          2. **Reference** *(Optional)* – Enter an internal reference number or note to help identify this quotation. This is useful for linking the quote to an opportunity, project, or campaign.

          3. **Quotation Date** – The date the quotation is created. This defaults to today's date but can be adjusted.

          4. **Doc. No.** – Automatically generated by the system after saving. You do not need to fill this in manually.

          5. **Expiration Date** – The deadline by which the customer must accept this quotation. After this date, the quote may be considered expired. This is typically set 7-30 days from the quotation date.

          6. **Payment Terms** – Select the agreed payment timeframe or conditions (e.g., 15 Days, 30 Days, Due on Receipt). This will apply when the quotation is converted to a sales order and invoice.

          7. **Pricelist** *(Optional)* – Select a pricelist to automatically apply special pricing or discounts for the customer. The selected pricelist will update product prices on the quotation lines accordingly.

          ![](/img/sales/quote/0004.png)

          Once you fill in the customer and other header details, the form will update to show the entered information.

          ---

          ### Step 5: Add Products or Services

          Under the **Order Lines** tab, add the products or services you are quoting to the customer. Click **Add a product** to add a new line item, then fill in the following fields for each line:

          1. **Product** – Choose the product or service being quoted. Start typing the product name and select the correct entry. This will auto-populate the description, unit price, and taxes.

          2. **Description** – Optionally modify or add a detailed description for the item. This appears on the printed quotation sent to the customer.

          3. **Quantity** – The number of units being quoted. The subtotal updates automatically.

          4. **UOM** – The unit of measure for the item (e.g., Units, Hours, kg, Pieces).

          5. **Unit Price** – The price per unit. This may auto-fill from the product or pricelist but can be adjusted to match your pricing strategy or negotiation.

          6. **Discount** – Enter a percentage discount to apply on this line. The subtotal will update to reflect the discount. This is useful for promotional pricing or customer-specific deals.

          7. **Taxes** – Select the applicable taxes for the product (e.g., VAT - Sales of Goods 10%). The tax amount is calculated and included in the total.

          8. **Subtotal** – Automatically calculated as: **Subtotal = (Quantity × Unit Price) – Discount**.

          You can also use:
          - **Add Multi Products** – To select and add multiple products at once
          - **Add a section** – To group related items under a header
          - **Add a note** – To include descriptive text or special instructions that isn't a billable item

          At the bottom of the Order Lines section, the **Total Summary** shows:
          - **Untaxed Amount** – The sum of all line subtotals before taxes.
          - **Taxes** – The total tax amount applied.
          - **Total** – The grand total including taxes.

          ![](/img/sales/quote/0005.png)

          ![](/img/sales/quote/0006.png)

          <Noted type="info" title="Note" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
          Carefully verify that all amounts, quantities, and pricing are correct. Quotations represent your company's professional image and pricing strategy. Review thoroughly before sending to the customer.
          </Noted>

          ---

          ### Step 6: Save the Quotation

          After completing all required fields, click **Save** to save the quotation as a draft. At this stage the quotation is saved but not yet sent to the customer — you can still edit it.

          ---

          ### Step 7: Send the Quotation to Customer

          Once you have reviewed and confirmed all details are correct, you have several options:

          #### Option 1: Send by Email
          Click the **Send by Email** button to compose and send the quotation directly to the customer's email address. The system will:
          - Attach a PDF copy of the quotation
          - Use your company's email template
          - Mark the quotation status as **Quotation Sent**

          #### Option 2: Print Quotation
          Click the **Print** button to generate a PDF version of the quotation that you can:
          - Print and deliver physically
          - Save for your records
          - Send through external channels

          #### Option 3: Mark as Sent
          If you send the quotation through other means (phone, in-person, etc.), click **Mark as Sent** to update the status without emailing.

          After sending, the quotation status will change from **Quotation** to **Quotation Sent**.

          <Noted type="info" title="Tip" image="/img/icon/notes.png">
          Always preview the quotation before sending to ensure all company branding, contact information, and terms and conditions are correctly displayed.
          </Noted>

          ---

          ### Step 8: Converting to Sales Order

          When the customer accepts your quotation, click the **Confirm** button to convert it into a Sales Order. This will:

          - Change the status from **Quotation Sent** to **Sales Order**
          - Lock the pricing and terms
          - Allow you to proceed with delivery and invoicing
          - Create a permanent sales order number

          You can also:
          - **Create Alternative** – To create a revised version of the quotation with different terms
          - **Cancel** – To cancel the quotation if the customer declines or the opportunity is lost
          - **Preview** – To view how the quotation appears to the customer

          ---

          ### Best Practices for Quotations

          **Before Creating:**
          - Verify customer contact information is up-to-date
          - Check current product pricing and availability
          - Review any special terms or discounts for this customer
          - Confirm applicable tax rates

          **During Creation:**
          - Use clear, professional product descriptions
          - Apply appropriate discounts transparently
          - Set realistic expiration dates (typically 7-30 days)
          - Double-check all calculations
          - Include relevant notes for special terms or conditions

          **After Sending:**
          - Follow up with the customer before the expiration date
          - Be prepared to answer questions or negotiate terms
          - Update the quotation if customer requests changes
          - Convert promptly when customer accepts

          ---

          ### Common Quotation Scenarios

          #### Scenario 1: Standard Product Quote
          - Select customer
          - Add products from catalog
          - Apply standard pricing from pricelist
          - Set 30-day expiration
          - Send by email

          #### Scenario 2: Custom Service Quote
          - Select customer
          - Add service items with custom descriptions
          - Apply negotiated pricing
          - Include detailed notes about scope of work
          - Set 15-day expiration
          - Print and deliver in-person

          #### Scenario 3: Volume Discount Quote
          - Select customer
          - Add products with higher quantities
          - Apply volume discount percentage per line
          - Add note explaining discount terms
          - Send by email with follow-up call

          ---

          ### Quotation Status Lifecycle

          Understanding the quotation workflow:

          1. **Quotation** (Draft) – Created but not yet sent
          2. **Quotation Sent** – Sent to customer, awaiting response
          3. **Sales Order** – Customer accepted, converted to order
          4. **Cancelled** – Customer declined or expired

          You can also create **Alternative Quotations** to present different options to the customer without losing the original quote.

          ---

          ### Frequently Asked Questions

          **Q: Can I modify a quotation after sending it?**
          A: Yes, but it's better to create an alternative version. If you must edit a sent quotation, notify the customer of the changes.

          **Q: What happens when a quotation expires?**
          A: The quotation remains in the system but should be marked as expired or cancelled. You can create a new quotation with updated pricing if the customer is still interested.

          **Q: Can I convert multiple quotations to one order?**
          A: No, each quotation converts to one sales order. If you need to combine items, create a new quotation with all items.

          **Q: How do I handle pricing negotiations?**
          A: Create alternative quotations with different pricing options, or adjust the discount percentages on individual lines.

          **Q: Can I attach documents to a quotation?**
          A: Yes, use the attachment feature to add technical specifications, terms and conditions, or supporting documents.

          ---

          For more details on managing the sales process, see the [Sales Order Guide](/sales/create-sales-order) and [Customer Invoice Guide](/accounting/create-customer-invoice).

          The Delete action allows users to permanently remove quotation records from the system. This is typically used when quotations are created by mistake, are duplicates, or are no longer relevant.

            Permanent Action: Once deleted, a quotation cannot be recovered. It will no longer appear in reports, analysis, or historical records.
          </Noted>

          Methods of Deletion

          ### 1. Delete Single Quotation

          ![](/img/sales/quote/0007.png)

          Users can delete an individual quotation directly from the quotation form or action menu. This is ideal for removing a specific incorrect entry while keeping other records intact.

          ### 2. Multi-Delete (Bulk Delete)

          ![](/img/sales/quote/0008.png)

          The multi-delete feature allows users to select several quotations from the list view and remove them in a single action. This improves efficiency when cleaning up drafts, expired, or duplicate quotations in bulk.

          <Noted type="info" title="Important" image="/img/icon/tip.png">
            * Only users with proper access rights can delete quotations

            * Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

            * Deleted quotations will be excluded from sales reports and audit records

            * System may restrict deletion of confirmed or converted quotations depending on permissions

            **Best Practice**: Use Archive instead of Delete when you want to keep historical data for audit, tracking, and sales analysis purposes.

          </Noted>

          tax).
