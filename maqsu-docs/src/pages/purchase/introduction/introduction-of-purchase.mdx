Everything you need to know about purchase orders

In this blog, we explain what Purchase Orders are, how they work, and how to create an efficient PO system that works perfectly for your needs. This blog post is for educational purpose only.

A **Purchase Order (PO)** is a formal document issued by a buyer to a seller that lists the products or services the buyer wants to purchase, along with details like quantities, prices, delivery dates, and payment terms.

It serves as an official request and contract between the two parties once the seller accepts it.

### **Purpose of a Purchase Order**

* Provides clear communication between buyer and supplier.
* Acts as a legal agreement once accepted.
* Helps businesses track and manage inventory and expenses.
* Useful for auditing and accounting processes.
