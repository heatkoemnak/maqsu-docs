Sending a quotation to a supplier is an official document that details goods or services provided, their quantities, unit prices, taxes, and the total amount due. In MAQSU ERP, sending a quotation to a supplier is crucial for tracking sales, managing customer payments, and ensuring proper financial records.

## Send Quotation to Supplier by Email

To send the quotation to supplier,

<Steps title="Open the Quotation" number="1" />

From the main dashboard, navigate to **Purchase** → **Request for Quotation** → Select **Quoation** that you want to send it to supplier.

**Step 2: Send Quotation to Supplier**

After reviewing and confirming the purchase request for quotation, click the **Send to Supplier** button to send the purchase request to the supplier. This will generate a PDF copy of the purchase request and send it to the supplier via email.

![](/img/purchase/6666.png)

**Step 3: Click Save**

Once you click Send Email, it will popup a window, that you will be able to check the company information and email that will be sent to the supplier. Please review and confirm the email details before sending it to the supplier.

The popup window will contain the following information:

* **Supplier Information:** The supplier's name, address, phone number, and email address will be displayed.

![](/img/purchase/7777.png)

**Step 4: Click Send**

Once you save, it will popup a Compose Email window that contains the purchase request details. This email will be sent to the supplier once you click the **Send** button.

The Compose Email window will contain the following information:

* **Subject:** The subject of the email will be in the format of "Purchase Request for Quotation - \[Supplier Name] - \[Document Number]".
* **Body:** The body of the email will contain the purchase request details, including the items, quantities, unit prices, taxes, and total amount.
* **Attachments:** The purchase request PDF will be attached to the email.

Please review and confirm the email details before sending it to the supplier.
