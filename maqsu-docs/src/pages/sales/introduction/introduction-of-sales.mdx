The **Sales Module** is the part of **MAQSU ERP** that manages your entire sales process — from creating quotations for customers to confirming orders, issuing invoices, and recording payments.

It helps you automate, track, and analyze every stage of the sales cycle within one system.

### Key Purpose

The Sales module helps your business:

*     Record and manage customer information
*     Create **quotations and sales orders**
*     Generate **invoices** automatically
*     Apply **taxes**, **discounts**, and **payment terms**
*     Track **deliveries** and **payments**
*     Integrate with Inventory, Accounting, and CRM modules
