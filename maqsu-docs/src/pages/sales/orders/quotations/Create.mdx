Here’s a short guide on how to create a quotation in MAQSU ERP 👇

**1. Go to the Sales Module**

* From the MAQSU ERP dashboard, click on Sales.

**2. Create a New Quotation**

* Click Create to start a new quotation form.

3 . **Fill in Customer Details**

* Select or create a customer.
* The customer’s information will automatically load.

**4. Add Products**

* Under the Order Lines section, click Add a line.
* Choose the product(s), set quantity, unit price, and applicable taxes.

5\. **Set Terms and Conditions (optional)**

* Add payment terms, delivery date, and other notes if needed.

**6. Save or Confirm**

* Click **Save** to keep it as a draft quotation.
* Click **Confirm Sale** to convert it into a Sales Order once the customer agrees.
