A **quotation** (often called a quote or sales quotation) is a formal document that a seller gives to a potential buyer, detailing the price, terms, and conditions for certain goods or services.

It’s usually the first step in a sales process — before an invoice or sales order.

Here’s a simple breakdown 👇

**🧾 Key Points about a Quotation**

**Purpose**: To give a customer an estimate or fixed price before they commit to buying.

**Not yet a sale**: It’s a proposal — the sale only happens once the customer accepts it.

**Contains:**

* Product/service descriptions
* Quantities
* Unit prices
* Total amount
* Validity period (e.g., “valid for 30 days”)
* Payment terms

**💼 Example**

If you run a printing business:

A **customer** asks how much it costs to print 100 T-shirts with their logo.

You prepare a quotation:

**100 T-shirts @ $5 each**

**Total: $500**

**Delivery time: 5 days**

**Valid until: October 20**

If the customer accepts, it becomes a sales order — and you proceed to produce and invoice.
