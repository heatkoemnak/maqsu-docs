To add a discount to a quotation in MAQSU ERP, follow these steps:

**1️⃣ Go to Quotation Page**

From the main dashboard, navigate to: **Sales** → **Quotations**

Then select an existing quotation or create a new one by clicking the “**Create**” button at the top-left.

**2️⃣ Open the Discount Section**

In the quotation form, scroll down to the bottom where you will find the **Discount** section.

**3️⃣ Apply Discount**

![](/img/sales/add_discount_01.png)

You can apply a discount in two ways:

* **Percentage Discount:** Enter the desired percentage in the discount field (e.g., 10 for 10%).
* **Fixed Amount Discount:** Enter the fixed amount you want to discount from the total.
