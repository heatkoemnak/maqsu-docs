If your customer has a **Tax ID** (for example, they’re a registered company that collects or pays VAT), here’s exactly how you should handle it in MAQSU ERP

![](/img/getting-started/customers/create_customer_05.png)

1\. Enter Tax ID

2\. Select Tax Type
* **Non-Taxable Person** is someone or a business not registered for VAT and not required to charge VAT on sales.
* **Taxable Person** is a company or individual registered for VAT with the General Department of Taxation (GDT) and has a Tax Identification Number (TIN).
* **Oversea Company** is a foreign business entity that is outside Cambodia and not subject to local VAT (in most cases).

3. Enter Customer Code
