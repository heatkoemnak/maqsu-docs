Setting up your \*\*Company Profile\*\* in \*\*MAQSU ERP\*\* helps personalize your business system with accurate information such as your logo, address, tax details, and contact info.

Follow the steps below to configure your company profile properly.

***

## Step 1: Access Company Settings

From the **Dashboard**, navigate to:

**Settings → Users & Companies → Companies**

![](/img/getting-started/company_profile/company_navigation.png)

This section lists all companies currently registered in your MAQSU ERP database.

## Step 2: Fill Company details

![](/img/getting-started/company_profile/company_details.png)

1. Enter Company Name
2. Upload Company Logo
3. Enter Company details

#### Optional: Cambodian Compliance

## ![](/img/getting-started/company_profile/cambodian_compliance.png)

Then click on the **Save** button.
