Logging into MAQSU ERP is the first step to accessing your business dashboard, managing data, and performing daily operations. Follow the steps below to log in successfully.

***

## Step 1: Access the Login Page

You can access the MAQSU ERP login page through your organization’s provided URL, such as:

![](/img/accessing_maqsu/browser.png)

## Step 2: Enter Your Credentials

On the login screen, you’ll see two main input fields:

* **Select Database** – Choose your organization’s database.
* **Email or Username** – Enter your registered user email or username.
* **Password** – Type in your secure password.

Once you’ve entered your credentials, click the “Login” or “Sign In” button.

![](/img/accessing_maqsu/login.png)

Once all fields are filled, click **Login** or **Sign In**.
