Managing user accounts in **MAQSU ERP** allows administrators to control access, assign roles, and maintain security across your organization.

Follow the steps below to create, edit, and manage user accounts.

## ⚙️ Step 1: Open the Users Menu

From the **Dashboard**, navigate to:
\
Settings → Users & Companies → Users
![](/img/getting-started/navigate_to_user.png)

This will open the list of existing users registered in your MAQSU ERP system.

![](/img/getting-started/list_users.png)
