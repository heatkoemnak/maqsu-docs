Creating a **MAQSU ERP** account allows you to access the system, manage your business operations, and collaborate efficiently with your team.

Follow the steps below to create your user account.

***

## Step 1: Go to the Sign-Up Page

You can access the MAQSU ERP login page through your organization’s provided URL, such as:

![](/img/accessing_maqsu/browser.png)

## Step 2: Fill Out the Registration Form

On the Sign-Up page, you’ll see fields to create your account:

![](/img/accessing_maqsu/signup.png)

* **Enter your Email** – Provide a valid email address.
* **Name** – Type in your full name.
* **Password** – Type in your secure password.
* **Confirm Password** – Re-enter your password to confirm it.

Once all details are filled in correctly, click the “**Sign Up**” button.
